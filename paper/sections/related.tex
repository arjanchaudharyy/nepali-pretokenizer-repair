\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Tokenization premiums.} Tokenizers trained mostly on English encode other languages in more tokens, so those languages pay more for the same content~\citep{rust-etal-2021-good,petrov2023unfairness,ahia-etal-2023-languages,lundin-etal-2026-token}. For Nepali, \citet{kadariya2026nepali} reports $4.79$ for \texttt{cl100k}, and \citet{srivastava2026tokenizertax} finds that \texttt{o200k} cuts the mean Indic premium from $8.0$ to $2.1$ without attributing the change to the regex. We measure sentence-level token ratios on parallel FLORES-200 text~\citep{goyal-etal-2022-flores,nllb2022}, following \citet{petrov2023unfairness}. Fewer tokens do not guarantee a better model~\citep{schmidt-etal-2024-tokenization,zouhar-etal-2023-tokenization,luitel2025perplexity}, so we also test the repair with continued pretraining.

\paragraph{Pre-tokenization of combining marks.} A 2024 tiktoken issue reported that GPT-2-style regexes split Indic words at vowel signs and proposed the class \texttt{[\textbackslash p\{L\}\textbackslash p\{M\}]+}~\citep{tiktoken_issue292}. ZeTT~\citep{minixhofer2024zett} adjusted its regex to avoid over-segmenting Hindi and Tamil, \citet{velayuthan-sarveswaran-2025-egalitarian} showed that the GPT-2, GPT-4 and Llama-3 pre-tokenizers break Tamil, Sinhala and Hindi, and \citet{rana2025mutant} cut Hindi fertility from 3.47 to 1.36 tokens per word by swapping the GPT-2 regex for Llama-4's; see also \citet{thakur2025artofbreaking}. \citet{regmi2026vowel} formalised the cause, stated the bound across 26 languages, and showed with 268M-parameter models trained from scratch that the wider class lowers Nepali bits per byte by 4.4\%. Qwen3.5's released tokenizer uses a mark-aware class~\citep{qwen35tokenizer}, a change also noted by \citet{land2026smallbugs}, and \citet{shrestha2026papaya} study morphology-guided pre-tokenization for Nepali in models trained from scratch. The edit itself is therefore known. What has not been studied is applying it to an already trained model while keeping its English tokenization.

\paragraph{Adapting the vocabulary of a pretrained model.} Vocabulary extension adds tokens, initialises their embeddings from existing ones~\citep{gee-etal-2022-fast,minixhofer-etal-2022-wechsel,dobler-de-melo-2023-focus,liu-etal-2024-ofa,remy2024transtokenization} and continues pretraining~\citep{cui2023chinesellama,tejaswi-etal-2024-exploring,kim2024eeve,csaki2024sambalingo,nag2024efficientcpt}; \citet{duwal2024nepalidapt} continue pretraining Llama-3-8B on Nepali with 4-bit QLoRA and without new tokens. Continued BPE appends new merges to a byte-level model's merge list; \citet{purason-etal-2026-teaching} introduce it and report tokenizer-only compression for many languages, Nepali included, under the unchanged regex. \citet{yamaguchi2026lowres} and \citet{yamaguchi2025elchat} extend Llama-3 and Qwen models for several abugidas, \citet{smith2026inplace} expand LFM2 and \citet{mehta2026defragmenting} Qwen3. \citet{didenko2026writing} adapts models to Ukrainian at fixed vocabulary size, keeping English token counts within 0.05\%. All of these keep the original pre-tokenizer. Tokenizer transfer~\citep{minixhofer2024zett,minixhofer2025alm} and grapheme- or akshara-aware pre-tokenizers~\citep{kapila2026typedriven,darshana2026wwho} replace more of the tokenizer. \citet{dagan2024getting} replace the tokenizer of a pretrained model, regex included, during code fine-tuning and need more than 50B tokens to recover; \citet{rana2025mutant} likewise give Llama-3.2-1B a new tokenizer with Llama-4's mark-aware regex and train on 40B tokens. Both alter English tokenization; ours does not.

The closest prior system is TituLLM~\citep{nahin-etal-2025-titullms}, which extends Llama-3.2 for Bengali; its released v2.0 tokenizer uses a \texttt{\textbackslash w}-based regex that keeps vowel signs attached. It does not identify the regex as a cause and has no regex-only or vocabulary-only arm, and its regex also changes digit and whitespace pre-tokens in English. We add a controlled attribution on deployed models and a repair that leaves every input without marks or Devanagari characters unchanged.
